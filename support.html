Before you start

  • Pushback runs on iPad in landscape, on iPadOS 16 or later.
  • The first career starts with a guided first day. A card near the bottom of the screen tells you what to do next. You can tap Skip guidance at any time, or replay the guided day later from Menu → How to play → Play the tutorial day (available before a day starts).
  • Everything saves on the iPad automatically. No account or internet connection is needed.

Day 1, step by step

  1. Read the briefing. Each day opens with a briefing that shows your goal, today's arrivals, any warnings (each with a fix button), your capacity, cash and daily bill. From Day 2 it opens with a Since Day N card: flights completed and on time, profit, reputation change, any licence earned, and why today's traffic is bigger or smaller.
  2. Tap “Start the day”. Traffic starts. Arrivals that are still off-screen show edge chips such as “NW120 incoming”.
  3. Land it. Drag from the plane onto the runway. Smooth, wide curves fly best. A red line means the turn is too sharp, so draw a wider approach. Planes need 80% of their size's runway length to land or take off, and the green strip shows where there is still enough room. Checking for traffic is up to you. A plane you don't route makes a second pass before it diverts.
  4. Park it. Keep the same line going from the runway to a free gate, or draw to a gate after touchdown. The line snaps to the taxiways. Roads are for service vehicles only. The label on the plane counts down “Park in”: reach a gate before it runs out.
  5. Let the services work. Baggage carts, fuel tankers and catering trucks drive out from their buildings on their own when a vehicle can reach the gate. Icons by the gate show passengers and bags loading. The top-bar chips show how many of each vehicle are free, and turn red when planes are waiting for one.
  6. Push back and depart. When the label says “Ready · draw to runway”, draw from the plane to the runway. A tug pushes it back and it taxis out. “Leave in” counts down to the push-back deadline and “Take off in” to the takeoff deadline. A flight is on time if its takeoff roll starts before the deadline. After take-off you can draw a new air route to send the plane off any edge of the screen.
  7. Stay safe. Each plane has a circle around it, and planes crash if their circles touch, in the air or on the ground. Amber and red rings, with a line between the two planes, warn you of a close call: amber several seconds ahead, red when you must act now. Tap a moving plane to hold it, or draw a taxiing plane backwards to turn it around. From the menu you can divert an airborne plane you can't fit.
  8. Use the speed controls. Choose 1×, 2× or 3×, or Auto, which drops back to 1× when something needs your decision. The pause button stops traffic.
  9. Finish the day. After the last arrival comes “Last departures”. The day ends when everyone has gone, or earlier with Menu → End day early. Ending early sends away planes that are still operating, and each one costs fees and reputation.
  10. Read the debrief. The end-of-day report gives a star grade out of five, the day's key numbers, the two biggest problems and one win, each with a one-tap fix where there is one. Full details opens the complete breakdown. If there was an incident, you can watch a 20-second replay and practise without affecting your career.
  11. Improve, then start the next day. Tap Improve airport to build, or Next day to carry on.

Building between days

  • Open build mode with the hammer button, Build airport on the briefing, or Improve airport on the report. Traffic is paused while you build.
  • Tools: Taxiway, Road, Gate (Small, Medium or Large), Remove, and buildings: Hangar, Fuel storage, Catering and Baggage depot. Each building shows what it is for and its price, and most can be upgraded.
  • Expansion cards: Runway extension (lets bigger aircraft use it), Second runway, North Terminal, Land west and Land east.
  • Pick a building and tap the map to place it. Connect every building's entrance to a road or taxiway. Nothing is paid for until you tap Build, and Undo works until then.
  • Vehicles (tugs, fuel tankers, catering trucks and baggage carts) live in hangar bays. Tap a hangar to add one, or open Fleet & fuel (the truck button, or the Vehicles button in build mode). Each job goes to the nearest free vehicle.
  • Fuel: Medium and Large aircraft need fuel. Build connected fuel storage, add a tanker, then order fuel before the day from Fleet & fuel. Automatic orders are available.

Progress

  • Reputation rises with clean, on-time days and falls with late flights, diversions and crashes. Below 50 it slowly recovers toward 50.
  • Licences: Training airport → Local → Regional → Network → Hub → Expert. A qualifying day completes at least 90% of its flights with no incidents, and later licences also need 75% on time. Career shows your progress on each licence.
  • Bigger aircraft: Medium flights can book from Day 3 at reputation 62, and Large flights from Day 6 at reputation 68. Visitors only book aircraft your airport can serve: a long enough runway, a connected gate of that size, and a fuel supply. If demand is waiting on an upgrade, the briefing says what to buy.
  • Traffic adjusts itself. It grows after clean, on-time days and eases after crashes, diversions or late days.
  • No game over. Mistakes cost cash and reputation. If cash can't cover the next day's bill and fuel, the next day is a quiet one with four Small flights so you can rebuild.
  • Early bonus: a plane that parks before its park-by time, or starts its takeoff roll before its takeoff time, earns 2% of its fees for every full 10 seconds early, up to 10%.
  • Restart day: Pause → Restart day, or Replay day on the debrief, returns the airport to how it was when the day opened. It is free, and cash packs you bought are kept.

Saving and backup

  • Progress saves automatically on the iPad.
  • Menu → Settings → Career & recovery has Export career and Import career (a file you keep yourself), Restart this day, Restore pre-reset airport, Sell airport & start again, and Reset airport.

Common questions

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Pushback plays fully offline. You only need a connection to buy a cash pack.

I bought a cash pack and the cash didn't appear.

Open Menu → Money → Cash packs and tap Refresh store / retry delivery while connected to the internet. The app delivers any purchase Apple has confirmed and never credits the same purchase twice. Cash packs are optional: everything in the game can also be earned by playing.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I move my career to a new iPad?

Either restore the new iPad from a backup of the old one, or use Export career on the old iPad, send the file to the new one (for example with AirDrop or Files), and use Import career there.

Will deleting the app delete my career?

Yes. Your career is stored only on the iPad. Export it first if you want to keep a copy.

Does Pushback collect any data?

No. There are no accounts, ads or tracking. See the privacy policy.
