Pushback: ATC Manager

Privacy policy

Pushback does not collect any information about you.

Home Support Privacy

Last updated 18 October 2026. This policy covers Pushback: ATC Manager for iPad, version 2.5.0 and later, made by Rakesh Wimmer.

In short

  • No data is collected, by us or by anyone else through the app.
  • No accounts, sign-in or registration.
  • No advertising, and no analytics, crash-reporting or attribution tools.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • Your game saves stay on your iPad.
  • Optional purchases are handled entirely by Apple.

What the app does not do

  • It makes no network connections of its own. Pushback plays fully offline. The only time anything goes over the internet is when you buy a cash pack, and that is Apple's App Store talking to Apple.
  • It contains no third-party code that collects data: no ad networks, analytics, crash reporting or attribution software.
  • It does not use the advertising identifier and does not ask to track you.
  • It does not use Game Center, iCloud, or any account system.
  • It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • It has no chat, sharing between players, web pages or links to outside sites.

Your game data

Your career (your airport, cash, reputation and progress) and your settings are saved in the app's own private storage on your iPad. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

  • Device backups. Like other app data, your save is part of your iPad's own iCloud or computer backup if you have turned backups on. Apple handles those backups under its own terms. The app does not upload anything itself.
  • Export and import. Menu → Settings → Career & recovery → Export career creates a save file only when you ask for it, and you choose where it goes using the iPad's share sheet. Import career reads a file you pick. Neither sends anything to us.
  • Earlier versions. If you played an earlier version of Pushback on the same iPad, the app can read that version's saved career from the iPad's local storage to carry your progress over. This happens on the device and nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Deleting. Deleting the app deletes its saves from your iPad.

Purchases

Pushback offers optional cash packs that add in-game cash. Everything in the game can also be earned by playing. Purchases go through Apple's In-App Purchase system:

  • Apple processes the payment. We never see your card details, Apple Account, name or email address.
  • The app receives a signed confirmation of the purchase from Apple, checks it on the iPad, and keeps a record on the iPad of which packs have been delivered so the same purchase is never credited twice.
  • Refunds and purchase history are handled by Apple.

Apple's handling of your information is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Information Apple may share with developers

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPad's settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give us crash reports and anonymous, combined App Store statistics, such as how many people installed the app. This comes from Apple under your settings. The app itself does not send it, and it does not identify you.

Children

Pushback collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data ever changes, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
